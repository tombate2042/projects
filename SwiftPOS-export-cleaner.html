Venue Export Cleaner

Shrink SwiftPOS price exports before they hit Sheets

Drop in a "Product Location Sell and Cost Prices" CSV export. This strips the columns you don't need and drops rows where an item isn't actually priced at that location — entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What exactly does this do?

Keeps 27 columns: Product Group, Category, Location, Description, PLU, Cost, Venue ID, all 10 Price columns, and all 10 Virtual Price Level columns.

Drops 12 columns that don't matter for pricing: INVENTORY_CODE, GROUP_NUMBER, PREV_COST, STOCK, UNITSPERCASE, CASES, UNITS, MINIMUM_STOCK, MAXIMUM_STOCK, TENPRICELEVELS, V_TYPE, SOFTWARETYPE.

Drops "dead" rows where every one of the 10 Price columns is $0.00 or blank — meaning that item isn't actually sold at that till, so there's nothing to reprice. This is usually the biggest reduction, since a report exports every PLU in your whole catalog against every location, whether that location stocks it or not.

Everything runs locally in your browser using plain JavaScript. Your files never leave your computer.
