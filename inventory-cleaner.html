Inventory & Promotions Cleaner

Build the Sheets inventory and promotions files from SwiftPOS exports

Drop in the SwiftPOS table exports and download the cleaned files for Sheets.

What exactly does this do?

Files are recognised by their column headers, so the filenames don't matter. Drop them one at a time, all at once, or drop the folder they're in. Products and Groups are used by both.

Inventory (Products, Prices, Location Stock, Groups) builds one row per item per price level, then:

  • Keeps only products in the drink group ranges (100–145, 149–182, 214–306, 310–410) and the mapped venue price levels.
  • Takes each row's cost and last-sold date from that venue's stock locations (location 132 is ignored). Last-sold dates are shared across size variants of the same product, e.g. 6089 and 6089 GLS.
  • Tidies categories into Spirits, Taps, Wine, Packaged and Non-Alc, with Type and Size split out.
  • Removes Spirits 15ml and Spirits 60ml, packs/cartons/kegs from Bar price levels, and pints/schooners/middies/7oz/jugs/30ml pours from Bottle price levels, plus Wine items coded GLS, 150ML or 250ML (by the glass).
  • Removes rows with a $0.00 price, and rows not sold since the cutoff date below.

Promotions (Products, Groups, Promo List, Promo Prices, and the cleaned inventory file) builds two linked files, joined on PromotionID:

  • Keeps promotions that are enabled, run on at least one day, have a name, and aren't food (names containing BISTRO, BISTO or FOOD).
  • Keeps promo items that aren't in food categories (10 and 25–69), on venue price levels ending in 1 or 2 (21, 22, 32 and so on), and that match a product by Inventory Code or PLU.
  • Removes duplicate items and any promotion left with no items.
  • Adds a blank NewPromoPrice column, and Category and Size for each item using the same rules as the inventory file. Anything whose description contains a hyphen, Worksmith or Vanguard, or that's in the Cocktail Serve group, is Category Cocktails, Size Serve.
  • Removes items that aren't in the cleaned inventory file at the same price level (except Cocktails), then any promotion left with no items.

The full rule set is in the CONFIG section at the top of this page's script. It can be edited in Notepad if the rules change.

The Prices and Location Stock exports are large (hundreds of MB). They're read in pieces rather than loaded whole, so expect a minute or two on a typical work PC.
